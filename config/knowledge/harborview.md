# Harborview Apartments resident policies

## Rent due date and late fees
Rent is due on the 1st of each month. A late fee of $75 is applied if rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the 5th. Late fees can only be waived by the property manager; the virtual assistant cannot waive fees.

## Ways to pay
Residents can pay by card or ACH bank transfer through the secure resident portal or a one-time secure payment link sent by text or email. For security, staff and the virtual assistant never take card numbers over the phone or by text.

## Payment plans
Residents who are behind can request a payment plan of up to 3 installments, with the final installment due within 60 days. The first installment must be at least 25% of the balance. Plans longer than that require approval from the property manager.

## Lease renewals
Renewal offers are sent 90 days before the lease ends. Residents can choose a 6-month or 12-month term. Accepting an offer creates a renewal addendum that must be e-signed within 7 days.

## Office hours
The leasing office is open Monday to Friday 9 AM to 6 PM and Saturday 10 AM to 4 PM. It is closed on Sundays.

## Emergency maintenance
For emergencies such as flooding, fire, gas smells, no heat in winter, or a lockout, call the 24/7 emergency maintenance line at (206) 555-0199. For fire or gas leaks, call 911 first.

## Pets
Up to two pets per apartment are allowed. There is a $300 refundable pet deposit and $35 monthly pet rent per pet. Breed restrictions apply for dogs.

## Parking
Each apartment includes one reserved parking space. Additional spaces are $75 per month, subject to availability.

## Tours
Prospective residents can book a self-guided or agent-led tour seven days a week. Tours last about 30 minutes. Bring a photo ID.

## Applications
Rental applications can be completed online in about 15 minutes. The application fee is $45 per adult. Incomplete applications are held for 14 days.
